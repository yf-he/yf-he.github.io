About Me

I am a PhD student in Computer Science at the National University of Singapore (NUS), advised by Prof. Bryan Hooi. My PhD is generously funded by the President's Graduate Fellowship. My research aims to build agentic AI systems that can improve themselves, learning continuously from interaction, feedback, and experience. To support this goal, I extensively research post-training for LLMs, to strengthen their reasoning and agentic behaviors.

I have published over 20 papers at top-tier (CCF-A) AI/ML conferences and journals, including ICLR, ICML, NeurIPS, and IEEE TPAMI, where I also serve as an Area Chair or reviewer.
